## **1. ARCHITECTURAL INTENT**
Chronos is a reinforcement-driven adaptive framework engineered to solve for fragmented liquidity and institutional slippage. It rejects commodity price-prediction heuristics in favor of **Execution Sequencing** and **Market Impact Optimization**.

Where **Apex Expert** dictates and **Sypher** secures, **Chronos** executes.

## **2. CORE ARCHITECTURE**
- **ChronosAI:** Deep Q-Network (DQN) architected for liquidity-sensitive decision logic.  
- **RLAgent:** Reinforcement learning agent utilizing experience replay and non-linear epsilon-decay exploration.  
- **ChronosMarketEnv:** A custom Gymnasium-compliant environment simulating institutional slippage, asymmetric market impact, and depth-weighted liquidity.

## **3. OPERATIONAL DIRECTIVES**
1. **Execution Prioritization:** Deterministic sequencing over stochastic guesswork.
2. **Liquidity Sensitivity:** Adaptive modeling of market depth and impact.
3. **Strategic Validation:** Real-time trade lifecycle optimization.
4. **Adaptive Governance:** Automated policy refinement via experience replay.
